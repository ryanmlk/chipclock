# Product Guidelines

## 1.0 Prose Style

The prose style for all documentation and in-app text should be **Direct & Concise**. This approach prioritizes clarity and efficiency, ensuring that information is conveyed quickly and effectively without unnecessary jargon or verbosity. The goal is to facilitate rapid understanding and efficient task completion for all users.

## 2.0 Branding

Our branding will focus on **empowerment and control along with Chipotle's current brand image**. This means the application's design and messaging will empower managers with robust tools for labor management, giving them greater control over scheduling and operations. Simultaneously, it will align visually and thematically with the established Chipotle brand identity, ensuring a cohesive experience for users.

## 3.0 UX Principles

The user experience (UX) design will be guided by **a combination of intuitive workflows, responsive feedback, and data readability**. This holistic approach ensures:
- **Intuitive Workflows:** Users can easily navigate the application and complete tasks without extensive training.
- **Responsive Feedback:** The system provides immediate and clear feedback on user actions and system status, enhancing user confidence and reducing frustration.
- **Data Readability:** Critical information is presented clearly and can be interpreted at a glance, enabling quick and informed decision-making.

## 4.0 Accessibility

Accessibility is a key consideration, with a focus on **Visual Contrast & Scale**. This includes ensuring:
- **Sufficient Color Contrast:** All text and interactive elements meet or exceed accessibility standards for color contrast, making content legible for users with various visual impairments.
- **Resizable Text Options:** Users can adjust text size to their preference without compromising layout or functionality.
- **Design for Readability:** Font choices, line spacing, and content hierarchy will optimize overall readability.
